\# Security Notes \& Portfolio



My working notes and portfolio artifacts from a structured, self-directed cybersecurity training program covering Web Application Penetration Testing (WAPT), SOC/Blue Team fundamentals, and GRC (Governance, Risk \& Compliance).



I'm a third-year Computer Science and Design Engineering student building toward entry-level roles in offensive and defensive security.



\## Structure



`notes/` — Day-by-day study notes, written in my own words, covering concepts, methodology, and hands-on labs as I go.



`portfolio/` — Standalone, interview-ready artifacts (e.g., a risk register) built from the daily work, not just raw notes.

\## Why This Repo Exists



I believe in learning cybersecurity by doing, not memorizing terminology. Every note here reflects something I actually worked through — a lab, an investigation, or a concept I had to reason through from first principles.



This repo will keep growing as I progress through WAPT, SOC, and GRC tracks in parallel over the next several months.
